  • Clean & spacious — easy to scan
  • Thin borders feel modern + refined

  • Less distinct from other crypto apps

    • 🪣 Quick Reference

    Theme Corners Borders Cards Vibe Best For
    📢 Sharp 8px (slight) 1px thin Flat, solid bg Clean, modern Daily drivers — Linear, Vercel
    🟢 Glass 16px (large) 2px bold Frosted glass + shadow Polished, premium Showcase — Vision Pro, Arc
    🔴 Term 0° (sharp) 1px colored Flat monospace rows Data-dense, power Terminals — Bloomberg, HackerNews
    🦢 Blem 20px (pill-like) 1.5px + glow Glass + neon borders Futuristic, loud Night trading — T-Bank, gaming

    🪣 My Verdict

    🤖
    For this app: a Sharp + Glass hybrid
    Take the clean layout of Sharp (thin borders, flat cards, 8px corners) as your base, but borrow Glass's elevation & depth cues for urgency and the neon urgency borders from Blem for critical alerts only.
    🟢 90% of cards = Sharp (thin, flat, 8px) 🟢 Critical/Urgent = Blem neon border + glow 🟢 Feed = 12px cards (room to breathe) 🟢 Token Detail = Sharp data rows 🟢 Settings = Glass cards (polished)
    Why: You get the usability and speed of Sharp for daily use, the wow factor of Glass for the feed experience, and the urgency signaling of Blem exactly where it matters — critical alerts. Best of all worlds.

    Or mix and match — every screen can have a different treatment
