Why it's one page

The *feed* and the *detail page* are identical DOM — the only difference is scroll depth and whether a star is selected. Scroll down = feed mode. Tap a star = detail mode (card slides under the sky, sky becomes the nav rail). Hit DEEP-DIVE on a card = the sheet extends the same page to full detail.

What "signal thresholds" means here

Notice the cards — some are *urg*, some *info*. The thresholds decide *which* events become cards at all, and which rise to *alert* color vs *watch*. In this build the rule engine (T2 rules-first + T3 adaptive) pre-decides: a 10× volume blip → PUMP card; a 5% supply move → RUGPULL-risk alert; only ambiguous cases touch the LLM for the *why*. That's what keeps it instant + cheap.

Interact

Tap a *star dot* in the sky → the card for that token appears beneath (Wode B, no navigation). Tap the *chips* to filter the feed. Hit *DEEP-DIVE* on any card → detail sheet slides up (Wode C). Tap *✕* to close it.
