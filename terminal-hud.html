Terminal HUD — Assessment

✅ STRENGTHS

  • Feels like a professional trading terminal (Bloomberg/TValley vibes)
  • Log-style feed makes scanning efficient — signal color immediately readable
  • Status bar gives live system telemetry at a glance
  • Keyboard shortcuts reinforce the "power tool" feel
  • Left accent rails are cleaner than full card borders
  • Data-dense without feeling cluttered — clear hierarchy

    • ⚠️ CONSIDERATIONS

  • Less visually expressive — prioritizes data over "vibe"
  • Keyboard nav only works well in a web/app context (not social media cards)
  • Log-line timestamps add noise if tokens update frequently
  • May feel too technical for casual browsing
  • Detail screen is information-heavy — needs good UX to not overwhelm
