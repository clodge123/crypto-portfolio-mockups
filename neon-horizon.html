Neon Horizon — Assessment

✅ STRENGTHS

  • Premium / sci-fi aesthetic — feels like a futuristic dashboard
  • Frosted glass + neon glow creates visual depth without clutter
  • Glow rings on token icons make alerts immediately visible
  • Gradient background adds atmosphere without distracting
  • Cards feel substantial without being heavy — glass effect carries weight
  • Good balance of "cool factor" and readability

    • ⚠️ CONSIDERATIONS

  • Backdrop-filter (blur) may have performance impact on lower-end devices
  • Neon glow effects can look overdone if not carefully tuned
  • Less "power tool" vibes than Terminal HUD — more about looking cool
  • Gradient backgrounds can make screenshots look inconsistent
  • Blur + glass effects harder to implement in Expo/RN vs pure CSS
