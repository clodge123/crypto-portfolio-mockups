Stage 0–0.4 — REGION LABELS (zoomed all the way out)

No individual stars. Just softly-pulsing region territory + a label for the dominant family (Meme-Corridor, DePIN Belt, Stablecoin Core…). A reaction at this zoom = the whole region label glows and the territory dims/strikes. This is the \"grab the vibe in one glance\" view.

Stage 0.4–1.4 — CLUSTER GYPHS (galaxy overview)

Each glyph is a bright dot, sized by member count, with a count badge + family label on the dominant city only. A pump makes one glyph swell + pulse + draw faint aim-lines to its nearest-neighbor glyphs; a rugpull makes it jitter with a red bloom.

Stage 1.4–4 — INDIVIDUAL STARS (constellation)

Glyphs shatter into member stars at screen-distance threshold. Stars drift around anchors, twinkle, and react per-token (the R2 archetypes: ignition / tremble / ring / echo / diverge).

Stage 4+ — LABELED STARS (deep-dive)

Ticker labels + HUD readouts appear past a second zoom. Search-snap pins a star here. Tap → card sheet drops in beneath (see §8.5 MODE 1/2).

MY CONSTELLATION (MODE 2)

Your held stars are always emphasized — louder + larger, with a warm halo — no matter what zoom you are at. At most zooms they also get constellation lines connecting them. They stay anchored in the one sky, so switching to mode 2 is just camera+emphasis, never a second sim.

REACTION AT EACH STAGE

Hit PULSE: SOLANA-PUMP or RUGPULL: MEME-SHIVER and zoom in/out repeatedly. Notice: the *same event* reads as a whole-region glow far out, one swelling glyph at overview, and per-star ignition/tremble up close. That is the depth-shifting reaction system working as one event.

How this maps to code (§11)

Server precomputes tokens→anchors + a quadtree of cluster glyphs (count, centroid, label). Client culls: off-screen glyphs skipped; glyph→star shatter threshold on screen-distance; label threshold on zoom. Reaction reach = V1 neighbor map (WHO) × V2 envelope (WHEN/HOW-MUCH); client just plays the decay. No LLM in loop.
